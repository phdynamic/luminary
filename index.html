Tips for good alt text
  • Describe what the image contributes to the lesson, not every detail.
  • Keep it short: about one sentence, under 150 characters.
  • Don't start with "Image of" or "Photo of"; screen readers already say it's an image.
  • Include any text that appears in the image.
  • Mark the image decorative only if it adds nothing to the content.
